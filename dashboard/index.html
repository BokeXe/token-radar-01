Method — how the number is built

The formula

implied = TVL × catMultiple × (TVL/ref)^β × growth × maturity × odds

  • catMultiple — median mcap/TVL of live-token comps in the category, fit jointly with the size curve by median polish and shrunk toward the global median with weight n/(n+4); categories under 5 comps use the global median 0.2138.
  • Size curve — β = −0.525 from the same median polish (slope of category-demeaned log ratios on log TVL); ref TVL $32,575,274 (comps median).
  • Growth — 1 + clamp(7d, −60, +120)% × 0.5. Structural, stated weight — growth does not predict the multiple.
  • Maturity — 0.6 + min(age, 1095)/1095 × 0.4; unknown age counts as 365 days. Structural, not fitted.
  • Odds — 1 − sigmoid(−1.2089 + 0.6141 × age in years), logistic fit on tokenized ~ age (n = 645). Falls with age: a protocol still pre-token after 3 years is unlikely to launch soon. Single cross-section, no launch dates — a model claim, not a measured hazard.

Launch odds by age

Share of protocols at each age that are still pre-token: 74% at 0–180 days, 60% at 180–365, 62% at 365–730, 45% at 730–1095, 21% at 1095–1460, 20% past 1460.

Farm economics

  • Capital tier by TVL: ≥ $1B $250 · ≥ $100M $150 · ≥ $10M $100 · else $50.
  • Gas per transaction by chain: Ethereum $8 · Bitcoin $3 · Solana $0.05 · other chains $0.50. Each plan budgets gas for its whole transaction count (3 + one per hold week) and keeps one exit transaction in reserve.
  • Hold: < 180 days old 60 d · < 365 d 45 d · older 30 d, across two weekly snapshots. Eight steps per protocol: wallet + gas budget, official-link setup, weekly logged action, hold, docs/points check, exit reserve, claim safety, verdict.
  • Expected farmer value = implied × 0.001% capture (1bp — a 5% airdrop split across ~5,000 farmers; change it in the sandbox). Implied already carries the launch odds, so odds are never applied twice. Verdict: Farm when expected value ≥ 20× gas, Watch ≥ 5×, else Skip.
  • distinct plan shapes across the board (category × chain × capital × hold × gas × verdict). Pin up to three rows on the board to compare them side by side.
