Method — how the number is built

The formula

implied = TVL × exp(intercept + category) × (TVL/ref)^slope × odds

  • Value if a token launches — median regression of log(mcap/TVL) on ln TVL plus ridge-4 category effects, fitted on the 94 traded comps of kinds the board can rank. Every term was tested held-out twice; growth, age and chain count failed a test and stay out.
  • Token odds — 1 − P(tokenized | age, size, growth, chains, category) from a ridge logistic fit on 383 dated peers (192 record a token). The peers' record, not a forecast: the page never says a token will launch.
  • Ranges — P25–P75 and P10–P90 from signed leave-one-out residuals, with walk-forward coverage printed next to the stated levels. Rank ranges: 5th–95th percentile rank across 200 bootstrap refits.
  • Unknown age counts as 365 days in the odds model; the snapshot reports no listed date for those rows.

Farm economics

  • Capital tier by TVL: ≥ $1B $250 · ≥ $100M $150 · ≥ $10M $100 · else $50.
  • Gas per transaction by chain: Ethereum $8 · Bitcoin $3 · Solana $0.05 · other chains $0.50. Each plan budgets gas for its whole transaction count (3 + one per hold week) and keeps one exit transaction in reserve.
  • Hold: < 180 days old 60 d · < 365 d 45 d · older 30 d, across two weekly snapshots. Eight steps per protocol: wallet + gas budget, official-link setup, weekly logged action, hold, docs/points check, exit reserve, claim safety, verdict.
  • Expected drop = implied × 0.5bp capture (a 5% airdrop split across ~10,000 farmers; change it in the sandbox). Implied already carries the odds, so odds are never applied twice. Verdict prices the work: Farm when net of gas pays at least $25 an active hour, Watch at or above $0, else Skip.
  • distinct plan shapes across the board (category × chain × capital × hold × gas × verdict). Pin up to three rows on the board to compare them side by side.
